# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Habit Tracker to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- being kind and patient, especially with newcomers
- respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- publishing someone else's private information without permission
- trolling, or any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/legend0x01/habit-tracker/security/advisories/new) or a direct message on GitHub ([@legend0x01](https://github.com/legend0x01)). All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who do so.
