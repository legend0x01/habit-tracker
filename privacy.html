Habit Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Habit Tracker is a free, open-source habit tracking app. This page explains what data it handles and why.

What we collect

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account. We use them only to create your account, show who is signed in, and keep your data separate from other users.

We store the habit data you enter (habits, categories, goals, check-ins and notes) so it can sync across your devices.

If you use the app without signing in, your data stays in your browser's local storage and is never sent to us.

Daily reminder settings (on or off, and the time) stay on the device where you set them. Reminder notifications are created by the app in your browser; no reminder data is sent to us or to a push service.

Where it is stored

Account and habit data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider. Database access rules ensure each user can only read and change their own data.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, use tracking or analytics cookies, or share your data with anyone except the providers needed to run the app (Supabase and Google sign-in). We only request basic Google profile and email access.

Deleting your data

You can delete habits and years in the app at any time. To delete your account and all of its data, open an issue on the GitHub repository and we will remove it.

Google user data

Habit Tracker's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
